# 👾 Space Invader (Ball Shooter)

A fast-paced arcade shooter where your goal is simple: shoot all the balls until they're gone to win! Defend the red line at all costs and test your reflexes across two distinct difficulty modes.

## Features
* **Two Difficulty Modes:** 
  * **Easy Mode:** A relaxed mode to practice your aiming and shooting.
  * **Hard Mode:** The challenge ramps up! The balls actively move downward toward your defenses.
* **Clear Win/Loss Conditions:**
  * **Victory:** Successfully shoot and eliminate all balls from the screen.
  * **Game Over:** A ball crosses the bottom red line.
* **Simple Mechanics:** Easy to pick up, but hard to master (especially on Hard mode!).

## Tech Stack
* **Language:** Java
